Diamond Sawing, Core Drilling & Robotic Deconstruction Services

From initial structural layout tracing to final carbon-plate reinforcement and waste haulage, Action Sciage delivers turnkey technical execution.

01 // ROBOTIC DECONSTRUCTION

Remote-Controlled Brokk Electric Robots

Engineered for industrial plants, mines, commercial spaces, and confined residential interiors where weight limits or tight access rule out conventional excavators.

  • Zero exhaust emissions for indoor, hospital, food-industry, and basement projects.
  • Remote wireless operation keeps technicians safely clear of falling debris.
  • Compact footprint with high hydraulic breaking and concrete crushing power.
02 // DIAMOND SAWING & CORE DRILLING

Vibration-Free Wall & Floor Cutting

Track-mounted diamond wall saws, floor saws, and core drills guarantee clean architectural edges and accelerated job-site timelines.

  • Load-bearing wall openings (up to 4 × 3 m+ industrial bays), doors, windows, and slab hoppers.
  • Variable-diameter horizontal, vertical, and angled core drilling for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical networks.
  • Turnkey core drilling at DIY rental parity (~€215 in Haut-Rhin).
03 // INTERIOR DEMOLITION & STRIP-OUT

Controlled Strip-Out & Debris Evacuation

Fast, tidy interior demolition tailored to occupied or downtown sites, backed by SEDDRe sorting and environmental compliance.

  • Block-splitting shear wall removal and phased urban structural dismantling.
  • Full waste sorting, container logistics, and BTP recycling traceability.
04 // MAPEI CARBON REINFORCEMENT & SS4 ASBESTOS

Carbon Composite Strips, Underpinning & Subsection 4

Trained and partnered with MAPEI alongside structural engineering offices, Action Sciage reinforces concrete slabs and beams immediately after sawing openings.

  • MAPEI CFK Carbon Plates: minimal headroom loss, ultra-lightweight handling, adaptable to curved/horizontal/vertical concrete surfaces, fast curing.
  • Underpinning & Openings: one accountable partner from saw cut to structural reinforcement.
  • Subsection 4 (SS4) Certified: regulated drilling and cutting on asbestos-containing panels.
