TECHNICAL FAQ // ALSACE & THREE-BORDERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does core drilling cost in Alsace (Haut-Rhin 68)?
For a Ø 200 mm / 20 cm core drill, Action Sciage offers a turnkey service around €215 (€75 drilling + €140–160 Haut-Rhin travel), matching DIY rental cost with zero deposit or breakage liability.
Which regions and border areas do you serve?
From our headquarters in Wittenheim (68270), we deploy across Haut-Rhin (Mulhouse, Colmar, Guebwiller, Saint-Louis), Bas-Rhin (Strasbourg), Territoire de Belfort (90), Franche-Comté (Besançon), Basel (CH), and Südbaden (DE).
Why use an electric remote-controlled Brokk demolition robot?
Wireless Brokk robots produce zero exhaust emissions, minimize vibration, keep operators safely outside the drop zone, and fit easily inside basements, hospitals, factories, and occupied buildings.
Can you cut load-bearing wall openings and reinforce the structure?
Yes. Working alongside structural engineers and MAPEI, we perform vibration-free diamond wall/slab cutting, underpinning, and lightweight carbon-fiber composite plate installation.
Are you certified for asbestos-containing materials?
Yes. Our technicians hold Subsection 4 (SS4) certification for controlled cutting and core drilling on asbestos-bearing building assemblies.
