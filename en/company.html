Young, Agile & Engineering-Driven Concrete Specialists

Founded in 2018 by Lucas Gaidella, SARL Action Sciage brings field experience and agility to diamond sawing, core drilling, and robotic deconstruction.

LEADERSHIP & INDUSTRY GOVERNANCE

Lucas Gaidella · FFB 68 & SEDDRe Leadership

Founder and Manager of Action Sciage, Lucas Gaidella serves as Board Administrator of the Fédération Française du Bâtiment Haut-Rhin (FFB 68) and President of its Demolition & Waste Treatment Professional Section.

Action Sciage is a proud member of SEDDRe (the French federation for deconstruction, decontamination, and recycling), guaranteeing responsible construction-waste sorting and environmental traceability.
